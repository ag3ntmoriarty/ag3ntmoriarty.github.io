Inspired from Steven Gong at https://stevengong.co to make conceptual notes about what I am working on in Obsedian. I believe instead of isolating concepts in respective domains one must understand how they connect with each other just like they do in nature.

I've generally followed a bottom-up approach to learning a new skill ie. creating a goal / outcome in mind and then working backwards to the current state, mapping out the technical details required to achieve the outcome.

### Note:
These notes are for presenting my understanding of the concepts and are therefore often incomplete. My aim to share these notes is to showcase the topics that I am interested in, and display my knowledge (or my lack of it) in a topic.
