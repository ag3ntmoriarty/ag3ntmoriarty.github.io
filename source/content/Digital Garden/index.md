Inspired from Steven Gong at https://stevengong.co I was inspired to make conceptual notes about what I am working on and the concepts required.

I've generally followed a bottom-up approach to learning creating a goal / outcome in mind and then working backwards to the current state mapping out the technical details required to achieve the outcome.

### Note:
	These notes are for presenting my understanding of the concepts and are therefore often incomplete. My aim to share these notes is to showcase the topics that I am interested in and display my knowledge (or my lack of it) in a topic.
